# Glossary

**Athlete**: a runner on the coach's roster.

**Grade**: the athlete's year in school, 1 to 12, as given when it was entered. squad
stores each grade with the date it was entered.

**Meet site**: the results site where coaches register athletes for meets and where
each athlete has a profile with their seasons.

**HS Grad Year**: the year an athlete finishes high school. The meet site's term,
which squad does not store.

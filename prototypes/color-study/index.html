Give color a job.

Violet: actions and selection. Blue: links and live information. Teal: successful assessment. Amber: attention. Rose: failed assessment or destructive action.

Make the text do less work.

14px reading and controls, 12px metadata, 24px titles. Brighter secondary text and clearer surfaces. Labels and symbols preserve meaning in grayscale.

Normal-text contrast target: at least 4.5:1. W3C contrast guidance. This study is not a complete accessibility audit of the product.
